In Use · 正文排印

Gswy Serif draws its character from the transitional tradition: vertical stress, moderate contrast between thick and thin, and bracketed serifs that sit firmly on the baseline. Every glyph in this family is assembled from a shared library of parametric strokes — stems, bowls, arches, diagonals and serifs — so the voice of the typeface stays consistent from A to z.

The design targets both display and text. A relatively large x-height keeps counters open at small sizes, while overshoots on round forms and a modest contrast ratio preserve elegance in headlines. Kerning pairs for the classic troublemakers — T, V, W, Y and A — are shipped as OpenType GPOS.
